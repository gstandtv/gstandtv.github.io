GrandStand for iPhone & iPad

Version 0.1.1 · First iPhone & iPad version: live sport, Live TV, Twitch, replays, and movies & TV with TorBox.

Apple only lets apps from outside the App Store onto an iPhone through a helper app called SideStore. You set it up once with a computer (about 15 minutes); after that everything happens on the phone. It's free and uses your own Apple ID.

You'll need

  • A Windows PC or Mac, just for the first setup, and a cable for your iPhone
  • An iPhone or iPad on iOS 16 or later, with a passcode
  • An Apple ID. A spare one is best (it's only used to sign the apps), but your own works too
  • Wi-Fi

1. On the computer

  1. Windows only: install iTunes (it lets the PC talk to the iPhone).
  2. Install iloader and open it.
  3. Plug in the iPhone. On the phone, tap Trust and enter your passcode.
  4. In iloader, sign in with your Apple ID, pick your phone, and choose Install SideStore (Stable).

2. On the iPhone

  1. Settings › General › VPN & Device Management: tap your Apple ID under Developer App, then Trust.
  2. Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode: turn it on. The phone restarts; confirm with your passcode.
  3. Install LocalDevVPN from the App Store, open it and tap Connect (allow it when asked). It never leaves the phone: it's how SideStore installs and renews apps without a computer.
  4. Open SideStore and sign in with the same Apple ID.

3. Get GrandStand

  1. On the iPhone, tap: Add GrandStand to SideStore
    If that doesn't open SideStore: in SideStore go to Sources › + and paste https://gstandtv.github.io/ios/source.json
  2. In SideStore, open Browse, find GrandStand and tap Get (LocalDevVPN needs to be connected).
  3. Open GrandStand. It shows this phone's device ID: send it to whoever gave you GrandStand, and it unlocks by itself once it's approved.

Keeping it working

  • Free Apple IDs sign apps for 7 days. SideStore renews them: every few days, connect LocalDevVPN and open SideStore (or tap the days counter next to GrandStand under My Apps). If it does run out, GrandStand just won't open until it's renewed; nothing is lost.
  • Updates show up in SideStore. Tap Update.
  • Keep Developer Mode on: apps from SideStore won't open without it.
  • A big iOS update can occasionally mean plugging into the computer once more and running iloader again.
  • A free Apple ID can have 3 of these apps at a time (SideStore counts as one).
